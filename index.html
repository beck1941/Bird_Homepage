Minnesota Bird Center

Welcome to the bird center of Minnesota! Located just off of the Mississippi River in South Eastern Minnesota, we are home to over hundreds of birds with both local birds and exotic. Our mission is to both spread awareness and to educate both the youth as well as the older generations to make people more familiar with the history of birds as well as the importance.

Minnesota is home to 446 documented species of birds. Our state bird is the Common Loon, which was named our states bird way back in March, of 1961. Minnesota is home to over 12,000 Common Loons which is the largest population in all the United States. There are five main families in Minnesota that you can learn more about above. Those include; Waterfowl, Upland Game Birds, Raptors, Songbirds, and Woodpeckers!

Birds appear all over the place in Minnesota culture. We see them in sports with our soccer team, at schools with mascots, and primarily in cities all over the state. Just about 50 miles from here, Rochester Minnesota, home of the Mayo Clinic, has their downtown centered on the Giant Canadian Goose. That is because in 1924, Dr. Charles Mayo brought in 15 Canadian Geese into his estate. About 20 years later a coal-fired power plant led to warm water discharge at Silver Lake. That led to the Giant Canadian Geese to stop migrating south in the winter eventually leading to their assumed extinction as they were declared extinct in 1957. However, biologist Dr. Harold Hanson rediscovered the species in 1962 as he was examining the flock in Rochester. Due to that the Giant Canadian Goose has become a symbol of the cities history.
